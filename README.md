# LeetCode and DSA

[View my LeetCode Profile](https://leetcode.com/u/__mxxrosh/)


I majorly code in Java and C++, and these days I am grinding LeetCode primarily in C++ as I prepare for software engineering interviews.

I originally started DSA because I knew it was something I needed to get good at for technical interviews. Somewhere along the way, though, I actually started loving it.

Now I genuinely look forward to sitting down with a problem, figuring out why an approach works, improving it, and sometimes trying to come up with multiple solutions to the same problem by myself.

This repository is where I keep track of that journey.

## What I am working on

I am currently focusing on strengthening my understanding of data structures and algorithms through consistent problem solving.

Some of the areas I am practicing include:

Arrays and Strings

Hash Maps and Sets

Two Pointers

Sliding Window

Binary Search

Stacks and Queues

Linked Lists

Trees and Graphs

Recursion and Backtracking

Dynamic Programming

Greedy Algorithms

## Languages

**C++** is my primary language for DSA and interview preparation right now.

**Java** is another language I work extensively with and have a strong foundation in.

## The Goal

My goal is not just to collect accepted solutions.

I want to be able to look at a problem, recognize the underlying pattern, reason about different approaches, understand their time and space complexity, and eventually come up with better or alternative solutions on my own.

What started as interview preparation has slowly become something I genuinely enjoy doing.

Still learning. Still solving. Still getting better.
